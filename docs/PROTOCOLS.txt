E2_day28_formulation_v1.0/PROTOCOL.txt
===========================================================================
E2 Day-28 landmark formulation experiment v1.0

范围：仅E2。E1保持只读，另存为E1_day28_internal_baseline_v1.0.zip并逐文件记录SHA256。
数据：直接读取E1的oulad_day28_features.csv；保持全部27421行、16个特征、原有训练/验证/测试学生划分和顺序。
Day28含Day0..28，共29天。E1已排除预测日之前退出及无法确认在读状态的记录，本轮不重新纳入任何人。
种子20261007；线程数6；软件依赖、预处理、搜索空间沿用E1。

E2A Merged：Success=0；Fail或FutureWithdrawn=1。
五模型LR、RF、XGBoost、LightGBM、CatBoost，每个30次Optuna TPE，验证集AP选参数。
阈值在验证集最大化F1；同E1，最终模型只在train拟合、不合并validation。
报告AP、AUROC、Recall、Precision、F1、MCC、BalancedAccuracy、Brier、固定0.5阈值F1。

E2B Flat：类别顺序固定[0=Success,1=Fail,2=FutureWithdrawal]。
五模型各30次Optuna，验证集三个one-vs-rest AP的算术平均（MacroAP）最大化。
选择MacroAP是对E1的AP目标的多分类扩展；附件未指定三分类调参目标，执行前固定此定义。
LR使用lbfgs原生multinomial，RF原生多分类，XGBoost multi:softprob，LightGBM multiclass，CatBoost MultiClass。
不改变搜索空间、不增加SMOTE、校准或测试集阈值调整。
三分类硬预测为概率argmax；所有类别概率列经model.classes_校验/排列。

E2C Dual：读取E1现有二分类模型，不重新训练、不重新调参。
主双路径：E1按各自验证集AP选出的CatBoost(W)+LightGBM(F|非退课)。
在包括Withdrawn的完整验证集/测试集上同时推断两个模型。
P(S)=(1-pW)(1-pF)，P(F)=(1-pW)pF，P(W)=pW。
三个概率之和为1；硬预测统一使用argmax，不使用E1两个二分类F1阈值进行路由。
报告原始pW、条件性pF、三个联合概率、预测类别、真实标签和row_id。
附加5组同模型家族的Dual：LR+LR、RF+RF、XGB+XGB、LGBM+LGBM、CatBoost+CatBoost，不新增模型训练。

选型与公平性：
主Flat按验证集MacroAP选模型；主Dual使用已冻结的E1分支选择，不按三分类测试结果重新选型。
因此主比较是两个预先规定的建模/选择流程的比较，不是完全排除了基础学习器影响的纯formulation因果效应。
同模型家族的Flat-vs-Dual作为辅助控制；搜索目标仍分别为Flat MacroAP与Dual两个分支各自AP。
本轮不为Dual组合额外搜索；Flat一个模型与Dual两个模型的计算开销不相等，应透明报告。
E1测试结果此前已被查看，本轮属于沿用冻结holdout的后续研究，不能称全新的盲测外部验证。任何观察后的新决策不得回写本轮主协议。

三分类指标：
AP_Success、AP_Fail、AP_Withdrawal为完整5485行测试集上的one-vs-rest average_precision_score。
AP_Fail不是E1仅在非Withdrawn人群计算的conditional failure AP，不能将两者直接比较。
MacroAP为上述三类AP平均；MacroAUROC为三类OVR AUROC平均。
MacroF1为labels=[0,1,2]的平均F1，zero_division=0；另报告Accuracy、BalancedAccuracy、MCC、LogLoss。
MulticlassBrier=mean_i sum_k (p_ik-y_ik)^2，范围0..2，未除以3也未除以2，越小越好。
混淆矩阵行是真实类别，列是预测类别；同时报告原始计数、行归一化和各类precision/recall/F1。

配对不确定性：
主比较4项：AP_Withdrawal、AP_Fail、MacroF1、MulticlassBrier。
按测试集id_student聚类，有放回抽取4955名学生；同一学生的所有选课记录携同重采样。
每次Flat和Dual使用完全同一组权重，记录Delta=Dual-Flat；主比较2000次，5组同模型家族各1000次。
95% percentile CI；固定已拟合模型、不重训、不重新选择模型；不包含训练和调参不确定性。
AP/F1的正Delta有利于Dual；Brier的负Delta有利于Dual。
区间是未做多重比较校正的点态区间；不据此宣称全局显著优势，也不以独立CI是否重叠代替配对分析。

核查：
E1全文件哈希前后一致；特征矩阵SHA256、split及测试row_id与E1完全一致。
训练/验证/测试学生交集为0；每种任务5模型且每模型30个成功trial。
Flat和Dual所有概率有限、非负且行和1，类别映射正确。
重新推断E1分支应复现E1保存的测试预测；所有三分类指标用保存预测独立复算。
加入合成概率组合测试、完美预测Brier=0、均匀概率Brier=2/3、相同模型配对差值=0检查。

不在本轮执行：其他landmark、跨课程/跨时间、UCI模型、SHAP、MOPSO。
附件中的文献数值、解释性推测和后续方案属于背景；未在本轮作为已验证事实。

复现（Python3.12，requirements-lock.txt）：
python run_e2.py --baseline ../experiment1_day28 --output . --trials 30
python audit_e2.py --baseline ../experiment1_day28 --output .
python build_report.py
相同代码和输入允许中断续跑；变更协议须使用新输出目录。e1_reference.py为E1训练辅助函数的原样副本。

E3_oulad_multihorizon_v1.0：执行前固定的实验协议

1. 范围与冻结
E1、E2只读；E2另存为E2_day28_formulation_v1.0。原文件逐一SHA256校验。
主分析Day14/28/56/84；仅Day14/56/84训练两个二分类分支、五模型、每模型30次Optuna，共900次新trial。
Day28使用E1冻结的特征、预测、阈值、模型与选型结果，不重训、不重新调参。
E3新增的nAP和Top-k在E3目录计算，绝不回写E1/E2。
不在本轮训练Merged/Flat或执行E4、UCI、MOPSO、SHAP。

2. 统一学生划分
master_student_split.csv涵盖原OULAD全部唯一id_student。
E1中存在的学生保留原train/validation/test assignment；检查E2完全一致。
其余学生先按数值ID升序，再用numpy.default_rng(20261007)打乱；前floor(0.6N)分train、随后floor(0.2N)分validation，其余test。
新增分配不使用标签；保存后冻结。所有landmark均查同一映射，不重新划分。

3. 风险队列与特征
直接使用E1 active_cohort与features_from_logs函数的原样副本，只传入不同cutoff。
观测窗口[0,t]包含两端，Day14/28/56/84分别15/29/57/85天。
依原顺序排除注册日期不明、注册晚于t、退出日期<=t、Withdrawn但退出日期不明、其他退出日期/标签不一致记录。
全部16个特征和零行为规则完全相同；日志在聚合前按0<=date<=t截断，并排除早于注册的日志。
recent7为t-6..t，previous7为t-13..t-7；不改变Day0处理。
标识符、enrolment_key、split、标签、退出日期均不是预测特征。
enrolment_key=code_module|code_presentation|id_student仅用于跨时点对齐；row_id仍为各时点局部编号。

4. 训练前门禁
先产生全部队列审计、特征、master split；执行独立审计后才允许stage=train。
要求：E1/E2学生映射完全不变；跨所有时点学生组交集为空；所有有效日志最大日期<=t；已退出者不进入风险队列；E1特征函数源文件哈希相同。
额外核查：各时点合成未来日志不影响特征；原始日志随机抽查及零行为/晚注册边界；Day28原特征文件按字节原样复制。

5. 模型和评价
W：未来Withdrawn vs其他；F：最终非Withdrawn中Fail vsPass/Distinction。
LR/RF/XGBoost/LightGBM/CatBoost、随机种子20261007、搜索空间、预处理均沿用E1。
每个模型30次TPE，验证集AP选参数；每时点每任务按验证集AP选择模型，不按测试集选择。
最终模型只在train拟合；验证集F1最大化阈值；不合并validation重训、不SMOTE、不后校准。
AP=average_precision_score；nAP=(AP-test_prevalence)/(1-test_prevalence)，允许负值，不裁剪。
nAP只作相对于当前阳性率的描述性归一化，不能完全消除prevalence依赖或case-mix变化。
报告AUROC、Brier、F1、Precision、Recall、MCC、BalancedAccuracy、固定0.5阈值F1。
主分析每模型AP 95%CI为1000次测试学生聚类bootstrap，固定模型；Day28复用原AP CI。
完整队列N/Pos/Prevalence与测试集N/Pos/Prevalence分别列出，避免不同分母混用。

6. 预先固定的容量排序指标
k=5%、10%、20%；选取ceil(k*N)条，概率降序，同分按enrolment_key字典序打破平局，不使用标签。
Precision@k=命中阳性数/实际选择数；Recall@k=命中阳性数/全部阳性数；Lift@k=Precision@k/该评价集阳性率。
主指标以学生—课程—批次记录为单位，不能把这些名额直接称为去重学生人数。
对Withdrawal另报告去重学生口径：各学生取该landmark合格选课记录中最大预测概率，标签为任何记录未来退课；同分按数值id_student升序。
该学生层分析也只表示风险排序，不代表干预能产生因果效果。F分支主分析仅在其条件性人群中计算Top-k。

7. Stable-cohort敏感性分析
R84中可能有Day14之后才注册的人，不能让其在Day14使用未来注册信息。
因此实际共同队列C=R14∩R28∩R56∩R84（按选课键），并核对等于R84且date_registration<=14；单列晚注册等排除数。
每个时点使用完全相同C测试记录，F再过滤Withdrawn。使用主分析已保存预测/模型，零新增训练或调参。
同时报告全部五个相同家族轨迹，主表仍用每时点原验证集选择，不在共同测试队列重新选模型。
该分析只固定评价队列，训练队列和最优参数仍随时点改变，不能声称完全隔离纯信息增益。
使用未来Day84状态筛选属于回顾性条件化，必须作为敏感性分析而不是前瞻主结果。
主分析跨时点delta为描述性点差，不能忽略评价人群变化。稳定测试队列的相邻时点AP/nAP差值另用1000次配对学生聚类bootstrap，固定已经选择的模型；这是未作多重比较校正的点态区间。

8. 交付
master_student_split.csv；day14/day56/day84_cohort_audit.csv与features.csv；Day28复制文件与复用证明。
cohort_summary.csv、split_summary.csv、40行全模型结果、两任务4点主表、AP/nAP/AUROC/Top-k轨迹、相邻窗口增益。
共同队列成员、各时点共同测试结果与配对差值；全部新模型、参数、trial、预测、审计、环境和输入SHA256。
不依据结果更改k、队列、split或主模型选择规则。叙述描述数据，不把不同任务AP直接解释成机制的因果证据。

E4A_C_source_only_calibration_v1.0/PROTOCOL.txt
===========================================================================
E4A-C v1.0: source-only logistic recalibration of frozen selected LR models
Scope: 14 outer module/task pairs. No base learner is retrained or selected.
Use only E4A's saved best-trial six-domain OOF predictions to fit each calibrator.
Outer test labels, predictions and prevalence never enter calibrator fitting.
All 14 calibrators are saved and hash-locked before any calibrated test scoring.

METHOD FROZEN BEFORE EXECUTION
z=logit(clip(raw_probability,1e-12,1-1e-12)); p_cal=expit(a+b*z).
Fit a,b by unpenalized binomial maximum likelihood on the pooled source OOF
rows, one equal weight per enrolment. This is standard sample-weighted
calibration, not a six-domain-equal calibration objective. No class balancing.
Use scipy L-BFGS-B with analytic gradient, b>=1e-8, to preserve increasing rank.
No method comparison, target prior correction, isotonic or threshold tuning.
Save optimization convergence and score-gradient checks. A lower-bound slope
or numeric rank changes must be disclosed rather than silently ignored.
Apply each saved calibrator unchanged to its frozen outer-test raw predictions.
Source-prior constant reference uses original outer-training event prevalence.

AUDIT
The OOF row keys/labels must equal the saved outer training membership, consist
of exactly six source modules, and share zero students with the entire outer
held-out cohort. Each OOF domain is checked against its frozen inner validation
membership; each corresponding inner training list must be student-disjoint.
All E4A file hashes are recorded and checked after the experiment.

EVALUATION
AP, nAP, AUROC, Brier, log loss; raw/calibrated/source-prior reference.
Calibration intercept and slope are the jointly estimated coefficients from
a diagnostic unpenalized logistic regression of TEST labels on logit(TEST p).
Those test diagnostic coefficients are never used to transform predictions,
select models, fit the source calibrator, or set thresholds. A separate CITL
intercept is estimated with slope fixed at 1. Perfect values: intercept 0,
slope 1. Diagnostic slopes are unconstrained. Constant reference has no slope.
Report domain-equal mean, population SD and worst Brier/log loss.
Per-domain paired 1000 student-cluster bootstrap CIs for calibrated-minus-raw
Brier and log-loss differences; fixed trained models/calibrators, seed20261007.
Negative difference means improvement. These are exploratory, unadjusted CIs;
do not treat the seven outer domains as independent samples for a macro CI.

RELIABILITY
Ten fixed equal-width probability bins over [0,1], including 1 in final bin.
Each task figure has seven module panels and an equal-domain-weight pooled
panel. Save nonempty bin N, mean probability and observed event proportion.
Empty bins are not plotted. Source fit and full-source refit score distributions
can differ; source-only calibration need not transport under domain shift.

E4A source-only hyperparameter selection is reused. OOF predictions were also
used to select those parameters, so calibration fitting has selection reuse;
outer test evaluation remains held out. No nested calibration-selection claim.
This is E4A-C plus E4B work only. E4B-SLR and later stages are not run here.

E4A_day28_student_disjoint_LOMO_v1.0/PROTOCOL.txt
===========================================================================
E4A Day28 student-disjoint nested leave-one-module-out v1.0
Frozen before training; only E4A is authorized in this run.

DATA AND TASKS
Byte-identical E1 Day28 feature table, 27,421 enrolments, 16 frozen predictors.
No new raw aggregation, selection or transformation. Feature availability audit
and cohort exclusions inherit E1. Existing split column is not a predictor and
is ignored: LOMO uses new domain-based splits, not the E1/E3 internal split.
W: Withdrawn versus all other outcomes. F: Fail versus Pass/Distinction,
excluding Withdrawn. Outcome is eventual status after Day28, not a fixed future
prediction window. Missing withdrawal dates and inconsistent records excluded
by the frozen E1 cohort. Source feature/code hashes must match.

OUTER SPLITS
Hold out all active Day28 enrolments in one of AAA,BBB,CCC,DDD,EEE,FFF,GGG.
Purge every held-out student from all other modules before task filtering.
Thus W and F share the same conservative student exclusion set, including
students whose held-out record would be excluded from the F task.
All 7 outer splits and all 42 domain pairs are saved before fitting.

INNER SPLITS
For each outer fold, each of the six remaining modules is validation once.
Purge all validation-module students from the other five modules, again using
the active cohort before task filtering, after outer-student exclusion.
Outer held-out data never enter preprocessing, hyperparameter or family
selection, threshold selection, or fitting. StandardScaler for LR fits only
the corresponding training subset. All other model/search-space settings
are byte-identical to frozen E1 via e1_reference.py.

SEARCH AND SELECTION
Five families in tie order LR, RF, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost; 15 fresh Optuna
TPE trials per family/task/outer fold, seed 20261007, no pruning or early stop.
Each trial fits all six inner domains. Maximize arithmetic mean of six APs
(average_precision_score), not pooled AP. Save each domain AP/nAP, mean AP,
population SD AP (ddof=0), minimum AP, mean nAP. First trial wins exact ties.
No E1 best hyperparameters are reused. All searches are independent.
Select family solely by best inner mean AP; exact ties use listed family order.
Freeze all 14 family choices before evaluating any outer test predictions.

THRESHOLDS
For the chosen hyperparameters of each family, retain six out-of-domain
prediction vectors produced during its best trial. Choose the threshold
maximizing unweighted mean F1 across the six inner validation modules over
the exact union of their probabilities plus 0 and 1. Predict positive at p>=t.
Lowest threshold wins exact ties. Fit each final model on all admissible
outer-source rows and transfer this inner-derived threshold unchanged.
Threshold-dependent metrics are secondary; AP/nAP/AUROC are primary.

REPORTING
Report all 70 family/module/task evaluations plus 14 validation-selected rows.
For every test module report N, positives, prevalence, AP, nAP, AUROC, Brier,
threshold metrics and enrolment-level P/R/Lift at ceil(5%,10%,20% of N).
Ties in ranking use lexical module|presentation|student enrolment key.
nAP=(AP-prevalence)/(1-prevalence); this is a descriptive baseline correction,
not prevalence-invariant performance or a causal adjustment.
Primary aggregate: seven modules weighted equally, population SD (ddof=0)
and minimum across modules. AP and nAP minima can occur in different modules.
Per-module AP 95% percentile CIs use 1000 student-cluster bootstrap draws,
seed 20261007, all enrolments of a sampled student travel together.
No bootstrap CI for a mean assuming independent modules: outer test modules
can share students, although each individual fold is strictly disjoint.
Optional secondary W capacity deduplicates students with max probability
and any-positive label; numeric student IDs break ties.

GENERALIZATION GAP AND INTERPRETATION
Gap=E1 internal metric - mean LOMO metric, separately for AP, nAP and AUROC.
Compare each family with itself; compare the validation-selected pipeline
with E1's validation-selected family. This is a descriptive protocol contrast:
training sizes, test populations, prevalence, tuning budget and validation
design differ. It is not a paired causal estimate of pure domain shift.
Model/module matrices, ranks and pairwise reversals are diagnostic only.
Never report an oracle selected on each module's test results as the pipeline.
Observed rank reversals motivate further testing but do not prove ensemble
improvement. No optimizer, Day56, temporal holdout or UCI experiments here.
E1-E3 remain byte-identical and are hash-checked before and after E4A.

COMPUTE AND RECOVERY
70 independent studies x 15 trials x 6 fits = 6300 inner fits; 70 final fits.
Three independent processes at most; each model uses E1's six CPU threads.
No GPU, network service or new dependency needed. SQLite database per study.
Completed study artifacts are skipped on rerun. If a study was interrupted,
move its incomplete directory to a work backup and rerun that whole study;
do not resume TPE with a reset sampler and silently change the trial sequence.

Base training populations and possibly selected families differ by horizon;

Dynamic-risk-set deltas are descriptive, not paired same-enrolment comparisons.

有效暴露日为max(0,date_registration)..28；每天无点击记为0。
